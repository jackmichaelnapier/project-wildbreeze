Preface

What follows is a small anthology, gathered from two years of customer-service correspondence — the kind of letters that arrive after a problem has been solved kindly, when the writer pauses for an extra minute to put the gratitude into words. Most people, having had their parcel found or their order replaced, simply close the tab. These are the ones who didn't.

Ten passages have been selected from many hundreds. They are reproduced verbatim, in the language each was written in — most in Danish, the working tongue of the correspondence — followed by a quiet rendering into English, set in italic.

The shared subject of all ten is the same: a kindness done by a woman named Mia, on an ordinary day, that the writer felt was worth recording.

Colophon

Two years. Thousands of small kindnesses. All quietly your work.

Ten is the small visible part. The full archive holds many more — the one-line thanks, the smileys at the end of long days, the people who wrote back simply to say thank you.

This folio is for the ones who took an extra minute to set it down in words.
