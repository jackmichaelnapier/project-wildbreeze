// 04Where the contact comes from

Almost every contact reason is dominated by email — the chat widget on the site is only 10–35% of inbound for any given topic. That changes where deflection has to live: the order-confirmation email and the shipping email matter more than anything inside Intercom.

Practical implication. Building self-serve forms is only half the job. Every form needs to be linked from the email the customer is replying to — the order confirmation, the shipping notification, the dispatch alert. That's where the customer is when they decide to write in. Putting a "Track your order →" link in the shipping email will move more volume than putting it in the site header.

// 05Action plan, ranked by impact

Items are ranked by estimated annual contact reduction with effort as a tie-breaker. Cumulative impact assumes the top six items launch — that moves roughly 50–55% of all customer-service contact either to self-service, to better products, or out of existence (deliverability fixes).

Estimates use 2025 + 2026-YTD pacing as the baseline. "Impact %" is the share of total real customer-service contact a fully-deployed item is expected to deflect — it's a planning estimate, not a guarantee.

#1 Form Order-tracking page (/track-order)
Self-serve page: customer enters order number + email, sees pre-ship / in-transit / delayed / delivered status, courier link, ETA, and a 'report missing parcel' button if late. Wire it into the site header, the order confirmation email, the shipping email, and as an Intercom auto-reply for inbound emails matching Re: .* pakke .* på vej / # 12... subjects.
Impact: ≈ 3,100 / yr Share of total: 25% Effort: Medium
#2 Infrastructure Email-deliverability audit (transactional)
'Order confirmation never arrived' grew +144% YoY — by far the fastest-growing pain in the data. Run Google Postmaster Tools + Mailgun/SendGrid logs against the last 90 days. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC are aligned. Check whether the from-address rotated or the IP reputation dropped. This is almost certainly a deliverability issue, not customer demand.
Impact: ≈ 900 / yr Share of total: 7% Effort: Low
#3 Form Claim form (/claim)
Order number + email + radio for issue type (missing item / wrong item / damaged / leaked / contaminated / other) + photo upload + description. Auto-creates an Intercom ticket with structured fields and the photos already attached. Won't deflect contact (these need human resolution) but cuts handle-time, sets clear SLA, and — crucially — gives you SKU-level data on which products fail in transit so packaging can be fixed once and for all.
Impact: ≈ 1,100 / yr (handle-time, not deflection) Share of total: 0% Effort: Medium
#4 Content Help centre / FAQ pages
About 20% of all real contact comes from pre-purchase questions where the customer just wants information. Top 20 articles to write: free-shipping threshold; is X gluten-free / vegan / lactose-free; protein-per-serving for top SKUs; delivery times by region; do you ship to Sweden / Norway / EU; how to apply a discount code; subscribe-and-save explained; how to track an order; how to return; what's in a private-label vs branded supplement; expiry dates and storage; jaw-trainer type 2 vs type 3 etc. Link prominently in site header and on PDPs.
Impact: ≈ 1,200 / yr Share of total: 10% Effort: Medium
#5 Form Returns portal (/returns)

Display return policy and refund timeline up-front so the customer can self-resolve. 70% of return requests already include the order number — they're doing the lookup work, you just don't give them anywhere to do it.

#6 Process Guest order lookup (kill the login wall)
Account-login contacts grew +111% YoY. A third of these messages already include an order number — these aren't password-reset requests, they're customers who don't actually want an account, they want their order. // 06How the numbers were built

Source: full Intercom bulk export (Preview API schema) of all conversations from 3 May 2024 through 3 May 2026. Exported daily to a private Google Cloud Storage bucket and pulled to a local machine for analysis.

PII handling

Before any analysis touches a record, the export is run through a local sanitiser: customer emails, names and phone numbers are replaced with stable hashed pseudonyms (so the same customer is recognisable across conversations without identity), free-text bodies are scrubbed for email addresses, phone numbers, IBANs and card numbers, and IP / device / geo fields are dropped entirely. Only the sanitised version is read for the analysis above. The raw export never leaves the local machine.

Filtering

About 29% of inbound traffic on the email channel is non-customer noise: out-of-office autoresponders to Price Mart's marketing emails, third-party review-platform notifications coming into the support inbox, and promotional auto-bounces. These are filtered before classification — counts in this report represent real customer messages only, not raw inbox volume.

Classification

Multilingual rule set covering Danish, Swedish, Norwegian and English, iterated against a hold-out sample. A single message can match multiple reasons (e.g. "my order didn't arrive AND the confirmation email never came"). Roughly 44% of messages don't match any rule and remain unclassified — these are typically very short widget messages or unusual one-offs and don't change the ranked picture.

Confidence and caveats

  • The classifier is conservative — when in doubt it leaves a message unclassified rather than guess. Real volumes per topic are likely somewhat higher than reported, not lower.
  • Year-over-year comparisons are between full calendar years 2024 and 2025. 2026 YTD is excluded from those deltas.
  • Impact estimates per action assume the form / page is wired into the appropriate transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping). A form that exists but isn't linked from email will deflect substantially less.
  • Conversation tagging in Intercom itself is currently empty for this account. All categorisation here is rule-based after the fact. If the team starts tagging, future reports can use those tags as a higher-confidence signal.
