This is where the headline narrative goes — usually three or four short paragraphs explaining what moved, why, and what to keep an eye on next. The content is generated from your data warehouse by a scheduled agent and reviewed before it lands here.

What we'd watch next

A short watchlist — usually 2 or 3 items. The point is to make the next decision obvious without making you read a dashboard.

  • Item one — short reason it matters this period.
  • Item two — what to look for in the next report.
  • Item three — anything that needs a human call.
Reports are short on purpose. If a number needs a paragraph to explain, it usually means the chart isn't right.
