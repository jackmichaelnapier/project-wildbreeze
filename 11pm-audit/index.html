The 11pm Spreadsheet Audit. A 1-page worksheet.

A printable worksheet that walks you through finding the recurring task in your operation that's the strongest candidate for an AI agent. Six questions. Ten minutes. By the end, you'll have a project spec good enough to bring to any specialist for a build quote.

Your email goes to the WildBreeze inbox. We won't sell it, share it, or send you sales sequences. Maybe one note from us when a major new article goes up. You can reply "stop" any time and we will.

What's actually in it

  • The six questions that separate "good first AI candidate" from "wait, this isn't actually structured enough yet."
  • A printable scoring rubric. Score your top three candidate tasks side-by-side.
  • A short list of common red flags (we'll tell you which kinds of tasks tend to look like good candidates but actually aren't).
  • A one-paragraph project spec template. If you can fill this out for your top scoring task, you're ready to go to any specialist for a build quote.

Who it's for

  • Operators of 10-50 person companies who suspect they have a task worth automating but aren't sure which one.
  • Founders who've heard about "AI agents" and want a way to translate the term into something concrete for their own operation.
  • Anyone who's been pitched by an AI consulting firm and wants to do their own homework before committing to a six-figure engagement.

What we do with your email

Send the audit. That's it. We don't have an automated nurture sequence. We don't have a sales team. If a major piece goes up on the field guide, we might send you one note about it. If you reply with a question about your operation, you'll get a real reply from Jack, usually within 48 hours.
