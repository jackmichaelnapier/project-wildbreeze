// MODULES — V1

The same plumbing, in named, drop-in shapes.

Every WildBreeze engagement ends as a piece of infrastructure running quietly in the customer's cloud. Over time, the same shapes appear: a daily briefing, a Monday report, a watch list of stock that's about to expire. We've named them. Each one has a fixed shape, a fixed scope, and the same rule — it lives in your cloud, it stays out of your way.

Eight pieces of quiet plumbing. Pick the one that fits.

Each module is a fixed shape, not a custom build. We deploy it into your cloud, hand over the keys, and step out of the room. The list grows slowly — only when a shape has been built three times for three different operators.
